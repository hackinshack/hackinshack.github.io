Now with multiplayer

Real curling, thrown by hand, in VR.

CurlingOne brings a full sheet of ice to Meta Quest — physical stone delivery, real sweeping, and real rules. Play solo against AI, or invite friends into a private match with voice chat.

Get CurlingOne on Quest See How to Play

Built for the sport, not just the idea of it

Delivery, sweeping, and scoring all work the way they do on real ice.

Full-motion delivery

Grab the stone, set your line and weight, and physically slide it down the ice — curl comes from how you release, not a canned animation.

Sweeping that matters

Sweep in front of a moving stone to change how far it travels. How you sweep actually affects the shot.

Real rules, enforced

Hammer, house, ends, and scoring work as they should, including the 5-rock, no-tick, and Doubles power-play rules.

Play your way

Play the classic 4-versus-4 Standard game, or the faster 2-versus-2 Doubles format. Short a player? Any open slot on either team can be handed to the AI, so you're never stuck waiting on a full roster.

  • Single Player — take on a full AI team, from Very Low to Very High skill.
  • Multiplayer — private rooms only, so you're only ever matched with people you've invited.

No public matchmaking

Agree on a room name with the people you want to play with, then create or join it together. There's no lobby of strangers — just spatial voice chat with the people you invited.
