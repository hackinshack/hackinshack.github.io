What it is

A roughly 24-foot sliding track that lets you deliver a real curling stone by hand. As it travels, the stone's path is tracked and projected on a screen — similar to how a golf simulator shows your shot, but for curling.

Where it's been

We've already brought the simulator out to local fairs, letting people try a delivery and see how it feels without needing ice.

Where it's headed

We're outfitting a trailer so it can travel to schools, parties, and bonspiels — pairing the hands-on experience with some of the physics (momentum, friction, spin) behind what's happening on the ice.
