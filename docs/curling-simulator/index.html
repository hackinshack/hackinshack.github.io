What it is

It's been through many iterations and has many versions, but basically it's a fun way to introduce the sport of curling, let people try a delivery and see how it feels to curl without needing ice.

Where it's been

We've had a few beers and laughs with it in the basement, taken it to local fairs, and a national championship. It's definitely a conversation starter, to say the least.

Where it's headed

Maybe somewhere near you, so keep your eyes peeled!
