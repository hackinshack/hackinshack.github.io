01Getting started

What do I need to play?

A Meta Quest headset and a Meta account. Multiplayer and voice chat need an internet connection; Single Player against the AI can be played offline.

Do I need to know how curling works?

No. CurlingOne is built to be approachable for people who have never seen a curling match — see How to Play for a full walkthrough of controls and rules.

Can I play by myself?

Yes. Single Player puts you up against a full AI team, with five skill levels from Very Low to Very High.

02Existing owners & pricing

I already own CurlingOne — do I have to pay again for multiplayer?

No. The multiplayer relaunch ships as a free update to your existing CurlingOne purchase, the same way any other Quest Store app update works.

03Multiplayer & voice chat

How do I play with friends?

Agree on a room name with the people you want to play with, then one person creates the room and everyone else joins using that same name. There's no public matchmaking or lobby list — you'll only ever be matched with people you've invited.

Will I get matched with random strangers?

No. Since rooms are only found by knowing the exact room name, you control who ends up in your match.

Is voice chat required?

Voice chat turns on automatically once you're in a multiplayer match with another player present. There's currently no in-app mute control — if you'd rather avoid voice chat entirely, play Single Player instead.

What if we don't have enough people for a full team?

Any open slot on either team can be filled with an AI player, so you can play Standard (4v4) or Doubles (2v2) without needing a full roster of humans.

04The new update

Do I have to use the new arena and visuals?

No. An environment picker lets you switch between the original scene and the new realistic arena, so if you prefer the look of the game you already know, you can keep it.

Does the environment choice affect gameplay?

No — it's purely visual. Ice physics, stones, and scoring work the same regardless of which environment you pick.

05Privacy & data

What information does CurlingOne collect?

An anonymous player ID, your Meta account association, room names, and match data needed to run a session — nothing is used for advertising, and we never see your payment details. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
