Support

Running into a bug, have a question, or just want to tell us something? Here's how to reach us.

Email us
[support email]

Before you reach out

Quick answers to common questions live in the FAQ, and if you're stuck on controls or rules, How to Play covers the whole game step by step. If neither answers your question, email us — we read every message.

Reporting a bug

The more detail you can give us, the faster we can track down what happened. If you're able, include:

  • What you were doing when it happened (delivering a stone, sweeping, joining a room, etc.)
  • Whether you were in Single Player or Multiplayer, and Standard or Doubles
  • If it was a multiplayer match, roughly how many players were connected
  • Anything on screen at the time — a screenshot or short recording helps a lot

Purchases & billing

CurlingOne is sold through the Meta Quest Store, and Meta handles all payment processing — we don't have access to your payment details and can't issue refunds directly. For purchase issues, refunds, or billing questions, contact [Meta Quest Support link].

Feedback & ideas

Suggestions, feature requests, and general feedback are always welcome at the email above. We can't promise every idea makes it in, but we do read all of it.
