01Who we are

CurlingOne is a multiplayer virtual reality curling game developed by [Studio/Publisher Name] for Meta Quest headsets. Net proceeds from CurlingOne support [Nonprofit Name]. This policy covers the information CurlingOne itself collects — it doesn't cover Meta's own platform-level data practices, which are governed by Meta's own privacy policy for the Horizon OS and Quest Store.

02Information we collect

In short: we don't sell your data, and we don't collect more than we need to run the game. Nothing here is used for advertising, and we never see your payment details.

CurlingOne is built to need as little personal information as possible. Here's specifically what passes through the game:

Account and identity information

  • An anonymous player ID, issued automatically when you first launch the game (via Unity Authentication) — this isn't tied to your name or email, it's a random identifier used to keep your session and settings consistent.
  • Your Meta account association, used to verify you're entitled to play the game you purchased (a standard Quest Store requirement) and, where applicable, your Meta display name shown to other players.
  • The in-game screen name and team/handle colors you choose.

Multiplayer and networking data

CurlingOne doesn't use public or automated matchmaking — there's no central lobby that pairs you with strangers. To play Multiplayer, you and the people you want to play with agree on a shared room name beforehand and meet there, so you're only ever matched with people you've already arranged to play with.

  • Room or lobby names you create or join, and your connection's IP address, which is used transiently to route multiplayer traffic through our networking provider (Unity Relay) — this is standard for any real-time multiplayer connection and isn't stored by us beyond the session.
  • Match data needed to run a game: team assignments, positions, scores, and shot outcomes for the current session.

Voice chat

If you play Multiplayer, CurlingOne uses Vivox (a Unity-provided voice chat service) to transmit live voice audio between players in the same match, for the duration of that match. We do not record, store, or listen to voice chat ourselves. Voice chat only activates for Multiplayer sessions with another player present — it is not used in Single Player.

Gameplay data

Basic gameplay information — such as game mode, format (Standard/Doubles), and in-app preferences like tutorial hints — is used to run the current session and may be saved locally on your headset so your settings persist between plays.

Purchases

CurlingOne is sold through the Meta Quest Store. Meta handles the purchase transaction, including any payment information — we never receive or store your payment details.

03How we use your information

  • To connect you with other players and run multiplayer matches, including voice chat.
  • To save your in-game settings and preferences between sessions.
  • To operate core gameplay features — scoring, matchmaking, team/position assignment.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs or crashes, using only the technical information necessary to do so.
  • To meet Meta Quest Store requirements, such as verifying game ownership.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

05Data sharing and disclosure

We don't sell your information. We share it only in the following circumstances:

  • With the service providers listed above, strictly to operate multiplayer, voice chat, and platform integration.
  • With other players in your match, limited to what's needed to play together — screen name, team, and live voice audio during that session.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety and integrity of our players and service.

06Data retention

Match and networking data exists only for the duration of a session and is not retained afterward. Locally saved preferences remain on your headset until you uninstall the game or clear its data. If we introduce persistent cloud-saved statistics in the future, this policy will be updated to describe what's retained and for how long.

07Children's privacy

CurlingOne is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Meta Quest devices and the Quest Store have their own age requirements and parental controls, which apply independently of this policy.

08Your rights and choices

  • Voice chat. You can play Single Player against the AI to avoid voice chat entirely.
  • Uninstalling. Removing the game from your headset deletes any locally stored preferences.

09Security

We rely on the security practices of Unity Gaming Services and Meta's platform for the infrastructure CurlingOne runs on, and we only request the minimum information needed to operate the game. No online service is completely immune to risk, but we don't collect the kind of sensitive personal data that would make CurlingOne a meaningful target on its own.

10Changes to this policy

If we change what information we collect or how we use it, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be called out clearly rather than folded in silently.
