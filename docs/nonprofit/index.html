Giving Back

Net proceeds from CurlingOne support a nonprofit — here's the idea behind that, and who it supports.

Why

CurlingOne exists to get people curious about curling — including plenty of players who've never seen a match, let alone thrown a stone. We've tried to keep that goal in view everywhere: how the game teaches you to play, how it's priced, and how it makes money.

Rather than treating the game as pure profit, net proceeds go toward a nonprofit cause. It's a way to make a niche sport's biggest, most accessible showcase also do some good beyond the game itself.
