About Us

Curling to learn.

Who we are

Hackin' Shack is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, dedicated to learning. Our goal is to encourage people of all ages and backgrounds to learn — about curling, and other stuff too.

Our story

Two paths converged: a passion for education, and a love of curling. We've taught in science classrooms at all levels. And then a bucket list trip introduced us to curling. And then, some questions. Why does the stone curl? How does sweeping work? Will my wife let me build a curling simulator in the basement?

Over the next few years, the simulator became a reality and now we take it on the road to spark community interest in curling. We continue to develop enhancements to the simulator and related programming, using profits from the CurlingOne app and via donations.

Mission

The mission of Hackin' Shack is to promote, encourage, and support educational opportunities, using curling as a conduit.
