PC companion app

Watch the match, no headset required.

CurlingSpectator is a free companion app that lets you follow a live CurlingOne match from your PC — join the same room the players are in and watch the sheet in real time.

Learn about CurlingOne

Built to watch, not to play

CurlingSpectator mirrors a live match — it can't affect the game, and it doesn't ask for much to run.

Join with a room name

Sign in with your Meta account and enter the same room name the players are using — no separate setup on their end.

Live, read-only view

Team assignments, positions, scores, and shot outcomes update in real time, straight from the match in progress.

No mic, no camera

CurlingSpectator never accesses your microphone or camera — you just watch and hear the match's in-game audio.

For the people who couldn't grab a headset

Not everyone in the room has a Quest. CurlingSpectator gives friends, family, or a crowd watching on a bigger screen a way to follow along with a match that's already happening — no purchase, no extra hardware.

Free to use

CurlingSpectator is provided as a free companion app. It doesn't process any purchase or payment information.
