Built for curlers, and soon-to-be curlers

Delivery, sweeping, and scoring work the way they do on real ice — and getting comfortable in VR doesn't take long.

Full-motion delivery

Set your line and weight, grab the stone, start your slide from the hack, and release. Apply a gentle rotation as you release the virtual stone to make it curl.

Sweeping matters

Sweep in front of a moving stone to make it travel a little further, and a little straighter. Sweep efficiency is effort-based, so sweep hard!

Real rules, enforced

The 5-Rock Rule, No-Tick Rule, and Doubles power-play rules are all in effect.

Learning the game

Check out the tutorial and the glossary of terms to learn about curling, in VR and beyond.

Getting around

Use the controller to move, rotate, teleport between ends, and follow the stone automatically for easy sweeping.

Ways to play

Play the classic 4-versus-4 Standard game, or the faster 2-versus-2 Doubles format.

  • Single Player — challenge an AI opponent. Select one of five skill levels for the AI player.
  • Multiplayer — get together with friends using a network connection to a private room you create yourself.

Short a player?

Play multiple positions yourself, or let AI fill open positions.
