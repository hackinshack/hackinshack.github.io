01About the app

CurlingSpectatorPC is a companion Windows application developed by Hackin' Shack, which lets you watch a live CurlingOne match — the Meta Quest virtual reality curling game — from a PC, without wearing a headset. This policy covers the information CurlingSpectatorPC itself collects. It doesn't cover Meta's own platform-level data practices, which are governed by Meta's own privacy policy, or the practices covered separately by CurlingOne's privacy policy for the headset app.

02Information we collect

In short: CurlingSpectatorPC is a view-only companion — it doesn't use a microphone or camera, doesn't sell your data, and doesn't collect more than it needs to show you a match in progress.

CurlingSpectatorPC is built to need as little personal information as possible. Here's specifically what passes through the app:

Account and identity information

  • Your Meta account login, used to verify you're entitled to use CurlingSpectatorPC and to link your session to a CurlingOne match (a standard requirement for apps that connect to Meta Quest experiences).
  • An anonymous player ID, issued automatically via Unity Authentication — this isn't tied to your name or email, it's a random identifier used to keep your spectating session consistent.

Multiplayer and networking data

CurlingSpectatorPC connects to the same room-based networking used by CurlingOne — there's no public spectator directory. To watch a match, you join using the room name the players have shared with you.

  • The room name you join, and your connection's IP address, which is used transiently to route traffic through our networking provider (Unity Relay) — this is standard for any real-time connection and isn't stored by us beyond the session.
  • Live match data needed to render the spectator view: team assignments, positions, scores, and shot outcomes for the match you're watching. CurlingSpectatorPC only receives this data — it cannot affect gameplay.

Voice and camera

CurlingSpectatorPC does not access your microphone or camera, and it does not transmit any audio or video from your PC. You hear the match's in-game audio only.

Purchases

CurlingSpectatorPC is provided as a free companion application. It does not process any purchase or payment information.

03How we use your information

  • To connect you to the CurlingOne match you're spectating and render the live view.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs or crashes, using only the technical information necessary to do so.
  • To verify you're entitled to use the app, via your Meta account.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your information to anyone.

04Third-party services

CurlingSpectatorPC is built on Unity Gaming Services and connects to the Meta platform for account verification. These providers process certain data on our behalf to make spectating work, under their own privacy and security terms:

ServiceProviderPurpose
AuthenticationUnity Gaming ServicesAnonymous player identification
Relay & LobbyUnity Gaming ServicesJoining a match's room and receiving live match data
Platform SDKMetaAccount entitlement and login

We encourage you to review Unity's privacy policy and Meta's privacy policy for details on how each of them separately handles data.

05Data sharing and disclosure

We don't sell your information. We share it only in the following circumstances:

  • With the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the connection to a match.
  • With the players in the match you're spectating, limited to the fact that a spectator has joined the room.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety and integrity of our players and service.

06Data retention

Match and networking data exists only for the duration of a spectating session and is not retained afterward.

07Children's privacy

CurlingSpectatorPC is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Meta's own account requirements and parental controls apply independently of this policy.

08Your rights and choices

  • No always-on access. CurlingSpectatorPC only connects while you're actively watching a match — it does not run or collect data in the background.

09Security

We rely on the security practices of Unity Gaming Services and Meta's platform for the infrastructure CurlingSpectatorPC runs on, and we only request the minimum information needed to operate the app. No online service is completely immune to risk, but we don't collect the kind of sensitive personal data that would make CurlingSpectatorPC a meaningful target on its own.

10Changes to this policy

If we change what information we collect or how we use it, we'll update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be called out clearly rather than folded in silently.
