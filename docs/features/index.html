CurlingOne

Features

A full sheet of ice, real curling rules, and the people you invite — built for Meta Quest.

Real curling, faithfully simulated

CurlingOne isn't an arcade take on the sport — the physics and rules are the real thing.

Full-motion delivery

Grab the stone, set your line and weight, and physically slide it down the ice. Curl comes from how you twist the controller as you release — not a canned animation.

Sweeping that matters

Sweep in front of a moving stone to shave off friction and change how far it travels. Effort is speed-based, so how you sweep actually affects the shot.

Real rules, enforced

Hammer, house, ends, and scoring all work the way they do on real ice, including the 5-rock and no-tick rules in Standard and the 3-rock power-play rule in Doubles.

Assists for newcomers

Toggleable aim guides — a virtual skip target and a phantom-stone shot preview — help new players read the ice before they're throwing blind.

Play your way

Two formats, and a game that adapts to however many people actually showed up.

Standard & Doubles

Play the classic 4-versus-4 game with a full lineup, or Doubles for a faster 2-versus-2 format.

AI fills any open slot

Short a player? Any open position on either team can be handed to the AI, in Single Player or Multiplayer, so you're never stuck waiting on a full roster.

Five levels of AI, adjustable on the fly

AI opponents range from Very Low to Very High skill, and any connected player can adjust the difficulty during setup — not just the host.

Multiplayer built for people you know

No public matchmaking, no strangers dropped into your match — just a shared room name and a full sheet of ice.

Private rooms

Agree on a room name with the people you want to play with, then create or join it. There's no lobby list, so you only ever match with who you've invited.

Spatial voice chat

Voice chat is positional — teammates and opponents sound like they're actually standing where they are on the ice, not flattened into one channel.

Your avatar, your rink

You show up as you — in a full arena built for the sport.

Your Meta avatar

CurlingOne uses your own Meta account avatar in the game world, so the people you're playing with recognize you, not a generic stand-in.

Custom team colors

Set your stone and house colors for the match, and CurlingOne remembers your preference for next time.

A stadium, not just a sheet of ice

Play in a full arena with crowd-filled stands — curling with the atmosphere of a real event, not an empty warehouse.

Getting oriented in VR

A long sheet of ice in a small room — CurlingOne is built to make that comfortable.

Guided tutorial

An in-VR walkthrough covers movement, teleporting, and rotating before you ever throw a stone, so you're not guessing at controls mid-match.

Teleport & auto-follow

Teleport between ends instead of walking the full sheet, and toggle auto-follow so your view tracks the stone automatically.
